Before you post

A good question gets a quick answer. These four habits help.

  • Search the boards first. Someone may have solved it already.
  • Say how you’re running Linuxium: live USB, installed, or in a virtual machine.
  • Paste the exact error text instead of describing it.
  • Be kind. Everyone here started out new to Linux.
