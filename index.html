Installing
Try the live session first, then install with Linuxium Experience

Put it on a USB stick

Four steps, about ten minutes.

  1. Download the latest ISO from the Releases page.
  2. Write it to a USB drive of 4 GB or more with balenaEtcher, or Rufus in DD mode. This erases the drive.
  3. Restart and open the boot menu (usually F12, F10, Esc, or Del), then pick the USB drive.
  4. Choose the live session to look around, or open Linuxium Experience to put Linuxium on your disk.

UEFI Secure Boot is supported. If the stick won’t start, check that your firmware is booting in UEFI mode.
